Common Symptoms

Detailed step-by-step articles for each symptom are coming soon. For now, each card links to the part category most likely to fix it.

🔋

Laptop Won't Charge

Charging light doesn't come on, or the laptop only runs while plugged in. Usually the charger, the battery, or the DC jack.

Shop Chargers & Adapters → ⚡

Battery Drains Fast Or Won't Hold Charge

Laptop dies quickly even when fully charged, or the battery percentage drops suddenly. Almost always a worn-out battery.

Shop Laptop Batteries → 🖥

Screen Is Cracked, Flickering Or Blank

Visible cracks, flickering, lines, or a black screen with the laptop otherwise powering on. Usually the screen panel itself.

Shop Laptop Screens → ⌃

Keys Not Working Or Sticking

One or more keys unresponsive, sticking, or the whole keyboard not registering input. Usually a keyboard replacement.

Shop Keyboards → 🔌

Screen Wobbles Or Hinge Feels Loose

The screen doesn't stay in position, or you hear cracking near the hinge when opening or closing the lid. Usually a hinge kit.

Shop Hinges → 🌬

Laptop Overheats Or Fan Is Loud

Laptop gets hot, shuts down under load, or the fan runs loud constantly. Usually a worn cooling fan or blocked airflow.

Shop Cooling Fans → 📦

Casing Is Cracked Or Broken

Visible cracks or missing pieces on the laptop body, palm rest or bottom cover. Usually a casing or palm rest replacement.

Shop Casings →
