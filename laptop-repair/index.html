What We Repair

The faults we see most often, and the parts we fit to fix them.

🖥

Screen Replacement

Cracked, flickering, lined or blank screen. We fit a genuine replacement panel matched to your model.

🔋

Battery Replacement

Laptop dies quickly or won't hold a charge. We replace it with a tested battery that fits your exact model.

🔌

Charging Faults

Won't charge or only runs on the cable. We find whether it's the charger, the battery or the charging port.

⌃

Keyboard Replacement

Keys not working, sticking or a whole keyboard not responding. We fit a compatible replacement, backlit where the model has one.

🔧

Hinge Repair

Screen wobbles, won't stay in position or cracks near the hinge when you open the lid.

💨

Fan and Overheating Fixes

Laptop runs hot, shuts down under load or the fan is loud. We replace worn fans and check for blocked airflow.

🧰

Casing and Palm Rest Replacement

Cracked or broken body, palm rest or bottom cover. We fit a replacement casing for your model.

❓

Not Sure What's Wrong?

Send us the symptoms and we'll diagnose the fault, so you know what needs fixing before you commit.

Laptop Repair Questions

How much does a laptop repair cost?

It depends on your laptop model and the part it needs. Send us the model and the problem on WhatsApp and we'll give you a quote.

How long does a repair take?

It depends on the fault and whether the part is in stock. We'll give you an estimate when you message us.

Which laptop brands do you repair?

We stock parts for HP, Dell, Lenovo, Acer, Toshiba, Asus and MacBook. Send us your model and we'll confirm we can help.

I don't know what's wrong with my laptop. Where do I start?

Message us what you're seeing, including any error messages or sounds, and we'll help you work out the fault. The Diagnose the Problem button at the top of this page also opens our symptom guide.

Can I just buy the part and fit it myself?

Yes. Browse the parts in the shop, or send your laptop model on WhatsApp and we'll confirm the right part before you order.
