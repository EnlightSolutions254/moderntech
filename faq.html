Ordering & Compatibility

How do I know this part fits my laptop?

Each listing shows exact compatible models. If you're unsure of your laptop's exact model, send us a photo of the sticker under your laptop on WhatsApp and we'll confirm before you order.

What if I order the wrong part by mistake?

Message us on WhatsApp before the part ships and we'll swap it at no extra cost. Once a part has shipped, our standard returns policy applies.

Do you sell parts for older or discontinued laptop models?

In many cases yes — send us your model number on WhatsApp and we'll check current stock, even for models not yet listed on the site.

Genuine Parts & Warranty

Are these genuine or compatible parts?

It's stated clearly on each product page — new OEM-equivalent by default, with genuine originals where available.

What warranty comes with these parts?

Most parts carry a 6-month warranty, listed on the individual product page. Warranty covers manufacturing defects, not physical damage.

What happens if a part fails within the warranty period?

Message us on WhatsApp with your order details and a description of the fault. We'll arrange a replacement or repair depending on the part.

Delivery & Payment

How fast is delivery?

Same-day within Nairobi for orders confirmed before mid-afternoon. 1–2 days to major towns countrywide.

Do you deliver outside Nairobi?

Yes, countrywide. Delivery cost and timing depend on your location — ask us on WhatsApp for an exact quote.

What payment methods do you accept?

M-Pesa is the most common, plus cash on delivery in Nairobi for orders confirmed on WhatsApp. We'll confirm the best option when you order.

For Technicians & Businesses

Do you offer bulk or trade pricing?

Yes — repair shops and businesses ordering regularly get volume pricing, priority stock holds and credit terms. See our Technicians & Bulk Orders page or chat with us on WhatsApp.

Can I hold stock before confirming an order?

For regular trade customers, yes. Ask us about priority stock holds when you set up a trade account.

Volume pricing, priority stock holds and credit terms for repair shops and businesses.
