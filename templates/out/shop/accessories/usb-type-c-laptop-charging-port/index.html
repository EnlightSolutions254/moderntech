Charging Port Questions

When do I need a new charging port?

When the laptop only charges if you hold the cable at an angle, the cable feels loose or wobbles, the charging light keeps cutting in and out, or the port is visibly bent or damaged. If the laptop doesn't charge at all, send us the details on WhatsApp first, because the fault can also be the charger, cable or battery.

What is the difference between big pin, small pin, USB and Type-C?

These describe the plug your laptop's charger uses. Big pin and small pin are round charger plugs with a centre pin, and they differ in plug size. USB ports take a USB-style charging connector. Type-C is the slim, oval connector. Look at the tip of your charger, or send us a photo of the tip and the port on WhatsApp, and we'll tell you which one you need.

How do I know which charging port my laptop has?

Look at the end of your charger. A round plug with a pin in the centre means big pin or small pin, depending on the size of the plug. A slim oval plug means Type-C. If you are not sure, send us a photo of the charger tip, the port and the sticker under the laptop on WhatsApp and we'll confirm.

Will this fit my laptop?

Charging ports differ between laptop models in shape, mounting and cable length, so one port does not fit every laptop, even within the same port type. Send us your laptop model, or a photo of the port and the sticker under the laptop, on WhatsApp and we'll confirm before you order.

Can I fit this myself?

The laptop has to be opened to reach the port, and on some models the port is attached to the motherboard, which is a repair job rather than a swap. Most customers have it fitted by us. Tell us your model on WhatsApp and we'll say what the job involves.
