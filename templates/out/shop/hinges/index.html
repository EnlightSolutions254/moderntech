Hinge Questions

How do I know if it's the hinge and not the screen?

If the screen won't stay upright, feels loose, or you hear cracking near the edges when opening or closing the lid, it's usually the hinge rather than the panel. Send us a short video on WhatsApp and we can help you confirm.

Does the kit include both sides?

Yes — each listing is a full left-and-right hinge kit for that model unless stated otherwise.

Can I fit this myself?

It requires opening the display bezel, so most customers have it fitted by us or a technician. We can recommend the fitting process on WhatsApp.
