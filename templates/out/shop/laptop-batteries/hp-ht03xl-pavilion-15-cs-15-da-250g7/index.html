Battery Questions

How do I know this battery fits my laptop?

This fits HP Pavilion 15-CS, 15-DA series and HP 250 G7 (also 15-DB, 15-DW, 14-CE, 14-CF, 17-BY, 17-CA, 255 G7). Note the similarly-named TF03XL uses a different connector and is not interchangeable — the part number on your old battery is the safest way to confirm. If you're unsure, send us a photo of the sticker under your laptop on WhatsApp and we'll confirm.

Is this an original or compatible battery?

This is a genuine original HP HT03XL unit.

This battery carries a 6-month warranty.
