      Charger Questions

      How do I know this charger fits my laptop?

      Each listing shows the wattage and connector type. If you're unsure, send us a photo of your original charger or your laptop model on WhatsApp and we'll confirm before you order.

      Is a higher-wattage charger safe to use?

      Yes — a charger rated higher than your laptop's original wattage is safe; your laptop only draws what it needs. A lower-wattage charger should be avoided.

      What warranty comes with a charger?

      Most chargers carry a 3-month warranty, listed on the individual product page.
