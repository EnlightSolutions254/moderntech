Cooling Fan Questions

How do I know it's the fan and not something else?

A loud grinding or rattling noise, or the laptop overheating and shutting down under load, usually points to the fan. If the laptop is silent but still overheating, it may just need cleaning and fresh thermal paste. Ask us on WhatsApp before ordering.

Will this fit my ThinkPad X1 Carbon?

It is listed for the X1 Carbon 2nd gen (2014) and 3rd gen (2015). The part numbers 04X3829 and 0C54435 are listed for the 2nd gen and 00HN743 for the 3rd gen, so send us your laptop model, or a photo of the part number on your old fan, on WhatsApp and we'll confirm before you order.

Does it come with the heatsink?

Yes, this is a CPU thermal module, which means the heatsink and fan come as one assembly and are replaced together. Fresh thermal paste is needed on the processor when it is fitted, and we do this when we fit it for you.

Can I fit this myself?

It requires opening the base cover and removing the old thermal module, then applying fresh thermal paste. Most customers have it fitted by us. We can talk you through it on WhatsApp if you'd rather do it yourself.

What warranty comes with this fan?

This fan comes with a warranty. Message us on WhatsApp for the current term before ordering.
